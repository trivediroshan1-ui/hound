Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Hound is an app where friends turn their step counts into challenges. This page says what the app and this website keep about you, what they do with it, and how you can take it back.

What we keep

  • Your account: your name, email address, username, and a profile picture or initials if you add them. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we get your name and email from them. We never see your Google or Apple password.
  • Your activity: with your permission, steps, distance, exercise sessions, heart rate and weight from Apple Health or Android Health Connect. These power challenges and your own charts. Hound only reads this data. It never writes to Health.
  • Your social side: friends and friend requests, the challenges you create or join, scores, messages and reactions on a challenge, nudges, kudos and Tic-Tac-Go games.
  • Your phone's push address, so we can send notifications you have turned on.
  • Ads you choose to watch: when ads are on, you can watch a short ad to earn Bones. Google AdMob shows the ad. It gets your Hound member ID and the date, so it can tell us the ad was watched, plus the usual details any app sends to show an ad, such as your device type and IP address. Hound never sends Google your health data, your name or your email.
  • Purchases: if you buy in-app currency, we keep a record of the purchase, such as the product, the date and a transaction ID. Apple, Google and RevenueCat handle the payment. We never see your card details.

What we do with it

  • Run the app: score challenges, show leaderboards and streaks, and show your friends what you chose to share with them.
  • Send you things you asked for: push notifications, and emails such as invites, friend requests and password resets.
  • Keep the service safe and working, for example by blocking abuse and fixing errors.

We do not sell your data. Ads in Hound are optional: you only see one when you choose to watch it for Bones. They are not personalized, so Google does not use your Hound activity or track you across other apps to choose them, and they are limited to content rated PG or lower.

Who else handles it

We use a small set of services to run Hound. They process data only on our behalf.

  • Supabase stores the database and signs people in.
  • Vercel hosts this website.
  • Resend delivers our emails.
  • Expo, with Apple and Google, delivers push notifications.
  • RevenueCat, with Apple and Google, handles purchases.
  • Google AdMob shows the optional ads and, in the EU and UK, asks for your consent first.
  • Google Fonts serves the typeface on this website.

What other people can see

Friends and people in your challenges can see your name or username, your initials or picture, and your scores and progress in those challenges. Your email address and friend code are not visible to other users. Messages on a challenge wall are removed after the challenge ends, once the retention period set for that challenge has passed.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, activity, friendships, messages and challenge scores are deleted. A few records may stay for a short time in backups. Invites sent to an email address, and purchase records we must keep for accounting, are handled as described on the delete account page.

Your choices

  • Turn off Health access or notifications at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Skip ads: nothing in Hound needs them. In the EU and UK you can change your ad consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices.
  • Delete your account in the app, or ask us to.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct it, using the contact below.

Children

Hound is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has an account, tell us and we will remove it.
